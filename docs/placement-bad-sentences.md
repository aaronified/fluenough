# Cards Find my level leaves out of its sentence questions

A sentence question plays or shows a target sentence and asks for its English meaning, so a card is used only when its target is a whole sentence in the course's script and its meaning is a whole English sentence. `lib/core/placement/sentence_gate.dart` decides; the app logs every card it leaves out (the app log, "Find my level"). This list was made from the decks on `decks-live` on 2026-11-14, so deck builders can fix the cards: a card fixed here is used by the next run.

Fixing a card means either giving a sentence target a full English sentence as its `native` (capital letter, end mark), or leaving a phrase as a phrase (it is then correctly left out). Reading decks' question cards are always left out; they are not listed.
