Language picker

The Add a language screen, 360 dp, 1.4x text. A first screen of nine big tiles, then the short list of languages that have a course, with how much of the course is made shown as one slim bar and the verification as a flag you tap. Picking goes straight to the existing Find my level (last phone, unchanged). Tiles, rows, flags and the Coming soon header are live. Notes and the contrast self-check are in language-picker.md.
