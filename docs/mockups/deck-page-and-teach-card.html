Notes

  • Deck page (#693). Every word is one row: the word and reading at the left, the meaning at the right with a small mastery chip (New, Learning, Known). The tag chips filter the list as they are tapped (Proposed a word shows when it has any selected tag; none selected shows all). The count line says how many words are shown. The per-skill buttons and Inspect are gone.
  • Two buttons, side by side, both on the filtered words. Practice opens a popup of skills with All (all on by default; unchecking All clears the rest; Start is off when none is ticked). Learn teaches the words not yet taught and shows how many; when every filtered word is taught it becomes Relearn, the guided walkthrough (#694). Tick a tag such as Calendar to see Learn become Relearn in the mockup (all of its words are taught).
  • Word sheet. A bottom sheet over the deck page with everything on the card, in the order: word, reading, IPA, audio; meaning, other meanings, accepted answers; part of speech and gender as one line; image; notes (exclamation icon); similarity warnings (sounds like, same spelling, differs by a dot or short mark, other meanings); the dictionary entry (Wiktionary senses and etymology, #692) and every example; tags, card id and Report.
  • Teach card (#691). The subset: word, reading, IPA, audio, main meaning, image, note, similarity warning, part of speech and gender, one example. Notes and warnings are separate blocks of the same shape; the note has an exclamation icon and the warning a triangle in the error colours. The same component fills the word sheet, which only adds blocks below it, so the three places cannot drift apart.
  • Card transition. Continue slides the next card in from the right in 200 ms (cubic-bezier .2, .8, .2, 1); the progress line moves with it. The slide is not a spring.
  • Proposed The mastery chips: New is an outline, Learning the secondaryContainer, Known the primary. The warning block uses the theme's errorContainer. The dictionary and example content is invented for the mockup (the real one comes from #692).
  • Readings are transliteration, based on ISO 15919. Text size is 1.4x only (owner, 2026-10-17). Hindi कल is a card with a base-word note: it means both yesterday and tomorrow.
