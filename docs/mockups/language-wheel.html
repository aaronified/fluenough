Language wheel

Dials Same units as the tab swipe: the settle is a spring (mass 1). Tell me the numbers.

The language bar's top row as a wheel (#703): the shown language is fixed at the centre and the row of glyphs turns under it like a tuning scale past a pointer. Only the top bar is drawn, nothing beneath it. Drag, fling or tap the wheels below: each is live. All frames are 390 dp wide at 1.4x text, the app's cap (#505), in the app's real theme (forest seed), light and dark.

Notes

What each variant demonstrates, from the spec (owner, 2026-10-21). Items I had to decide are marked Proposed.

  • All three. The wheel is centred in the bar between two 48 dp slots; the selection is a fixed pill at the centre and the glyphs scroll under it. Cylinder projection: a glyph at θ = offset × 16.8° sits at R·sin θ (R = 150 dp, so 1:1 at the centre and tighter toward the ends), is scaled 0.65 + 0.35·cos θ and has opacity 0.35 + 0.65·cos θ; both ends fade under a CSS mask-image. Looping: no ends, Korean is followed by Hindi.
  • Expansion (owner, 2026-10-21). As soon as the ratchet stops, the pill expands to the language's full name (UI language) in about 0.3 s, the neighbours fade out; a touch collapses it. Damping slightly increased: friction 2.4 (was 2.0), settle 16/s (was 14).
  • Two languages. They repeat round the wheel to fill it. The label under the bar names the language at the centre while moving, and stays for 1 s after it settles, as today.
  • Five languages, Progress. All at the left, bug report at the right; the wheel does not move when the screen changes (the empty slot on Today and Decks keeps it centred).
  • Ten languages. A long drag passes many languages at 1:1 finger tracking (pointer events). Each language is a detent: the pill pulses briefly and navigator.vibrate(5) is called where available, on every detent crossed in a drag and in a fling. The counter under each frame is the page's, not the app's.
  • Release. Momentum decays exponentially, velocity ×= e^(−k t), as Flutter's FrictionSimulation does. The stopping point is the nearest detent to where plain friction would stop (x + v/k), and the decay constant is adjusted to v/(target − x) so it lands exactly there: no overshoot, no bounce, and the same fling always goes the same distance. Proposed k = 2.0 per second (Flutter's default drag of 0.135 is e^−2 per second), so a 2000 px/s fling travels about 23 languages.
  • Touch to stop. A pointerdown during the coast stops it dead; dragging goes on from there; lifting without a drag settles on the nearest detent. Proposed A release that is too slow to coast, or that points back against the nearest detent, settles with a quick exponential ease (k = 14 per second), still without overshoot.
  • Tap. A tap with no drag, on a wheel at rest, shows the note “opens the language menu”. A tap that only stopped a coast does not open the menu.
  • Proposed The pill shows the glyph only (the others' bare glyphs are the wheel itself); the name appears in the label under the bar, which is shown while moving and for 1 s after, as in the built bar. The pill grows with text size as the chip does and is wider than a single glyph so Hindi's हि and Spanish's Es fit.
  • Proposed Japanese has no icon in decks/index.json yet, so its glyph here is the 日 of its first deck, as languageGlyph falls back to; the other glyphs are the course icons (हि, বা, తె, Es, म, ಕ, অ, 中, 한).
  • Proposed Keyboard: left and right arrows move one detent, so the wheel is usable with a switch or keyboard; the built version would also keep today's “next/previous language” accessibility actions.
  • Not mocked Right-to-left (the direction simply reverses), the language menu, and anything beneath the bar. Text size is 1.4x only (owner, 2026-10-17). Readings in decks stay based on ISO 15919, though none show here.
