The session result, counted right, and hours on Progress

Two screens, drawn only where they change. #486: the result after a session counts every card worked through, gives the share right over the answers that were graded, takes its minutes from the answers' own times (each at most 60 s), and its skill rows add up to the answers. #227: Progress shows the hours spent in the chosen range and the hours left in the decks written so far. Bengali throughout. Colours are Flutter's own ColorScheme.fromSeed output for the app's four seeds, as in languages-you-know.html; the default, Clay on pure black, is the owner's phone. The dashed outlines mark what changes and are not part of the app.

1 · The result after a session (#486)

A Bengali lesson of 27 steps: 9 new cards taught, then 18 questions (multiple choice, match pairs, rearrange, typed). Today's screen says “5 cards · 4 correct” and 31 minutes. After the fix the line counts every card, the percentage is right answers over graded answers (15 of 18 is 83%), the minutes are the 18 answers' times with each capped at 60 s, and Recognition and Write add up to the 18 answered.

  • Teach cards record no time (the plan's open question), so the minutes count answers only. Reading a passage before its questions is not counted, as for hours.
  • Proposed with it: above 1.5x text the skill rows put the skill's name on its own line, as the three numbers already stack there. At 2x, “Recognition” no longer fits the 110 dp label column and Flutter would break it mid-word.

2 · Hours on Progress (#227)

A third row of tiles, under today's four and above the activity grid, in the same large tiles. Hours spent follows the range chosen above (7 days, 30 days, All time) and the language chip: each answer at most 60 s, summed, to one decimal. Hours left in the decks so far uses the Decks course card's words: it counts only units whose decks exist, rounded up to whole hours, and grows as decks are added. It is what is left now, so it is the same in every range.
