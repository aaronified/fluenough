Languages you know: the bottom sheet, families folded

Issue #490 grows the list from 3 languages to 35. The owner chose the bottom sheet (2026-10-12): tapping a language opens a sheet that asks about its script in that script, so the answer that says “I can read it” can only be read by someone who can. The other answer, in the app's language, is “I can only speak it”. One tap chooses the language and answers for its script. Nothing is labelled Yes or No. Above the list: a one-line search, Your languages and the top picks. Below: the five families, folded. It is one screen, used at first launch (back, progress wave, Continue) and in Settings (app bar, back, Save); only that chrome differs. Colours are Flutter's own ColorScheme.fromSeed output for the app's four seeds. The default, Clay on pure black, is the owner's phone.

The sheet's words

The question and the “I can read it” answer are in the language's own script, each with a line in the app's language under it. The other answer is in the app's language only. Both Hindi lines use मुझे … आती है / क्या आपको … आती है?, “is known to me”. The verb agrees with लिपि (feminine), not with the speaker, so one sentence serves everyone. मैं … पढ़ सकता/सकती हूँ would need a form for each gender. The Bengali verb does not mark gender, and আপনি is the polite “you”, as the app uses elsewhere.

  • Hindi's script is Devanagari, which Hindi shares with Marathi, Nepali, Bhojpuri and others. “Hindi script” (हिंदी लिपि) is what people say; for a language whose script name is better known, such as Gurmukhi for Punjabi, the sentence names the script itself. Each language's three sentences are data in assets/languages.yaml, written and checked by a speaker, never put together from parts.
  • A language with no own-script sentences yet asks in the app's language only, with the same two answers.
  • Folding: each family is a row with its name, how many languages it holds, and an arrow. A screen reader hears “Indo-Aryan, 24 languages, collapsed, button” and then “expanded”. Searching shows every match whatever is folded. Your languages and the top picks stay above the families.
  • Tapping a chosen language opens the same sheet with its answer marked, plus “Remove Bengali”.
  • The sheet's own question is its heading: it already names the language. Its text grows at most 1.5x, as the first launch's question does, so both answers stay in view at the largest text size.
