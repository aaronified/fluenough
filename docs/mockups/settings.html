Settings, with every planned change, drawn once

The whole Settings tab, top to bottom, with what 0.5.0 and 0.6.0 plan for it folded in, so it is rearranged once rather than five times. #469 Sources moves from Logs to Your languages (the page once called Learning). #473 Your data and Back up to the cloud become one section, which holds #470 Backup and restore; cloud backup moves onto that page, built with it. #475 Add deck leaves the Decks screen for a row of Settings. #243, as the owner narrowed it on 2026-10-12: switches only for the activities a learner may skip, with the Sound section merged into them. #90 Reminders gets its page now and ships in 0.6.0. Languages I know opens the approved screen of #490. Everything else is as settings_page.dart draws it today, in its order, with its icons and words. The learner knows English, learns Bengali and Hindi, and has reviewing on. Revised 2026-10-12 after the owner's reviews: Settings is now a short list of section pages, with a Review log and an App log screen, destructive actions in the error colour, recent backups, cloud backup on the backup page, reminders for several languages without quiet hours, and fewer activity switches. Colours are Flutter's own ColorScheme.fromSeed output for the app's four seeds, as in languages-you-know.html; the default, Clay on pure black, is the owner's phone. Dashed outlines mark what changes and are not part of the app. Decisions this page makes are marked Proposed.

1 · The Settings tab: a short list of pages

Owner, 2026-10-12: “the settings is too long now.” The tab is now the profile card and one row per section, each with a one-line summary of what is set, opening that section's page. Every control left the tab. The list is about one and three quarters screens at 1x, with ten rows. Add deck stays a row of its own because it is one action and opens its flow straight away; App log opens the log itself. Rows marked by the dashed outline are new.

2 · The section pages

Each page is a pushed page, with back and the report button, holding what the section held: the same rows, icons and words as before, without a heading, since the page's title names it. Activities and memory also holds Adjust to me, which fits each activity's memory to the learner. Flip every switch, above, redraws them with every switch the other way.

3 · Your decks, and checking an upload

Owner, 2026-10-12: Add deck becomes Your decks: the learner's own decks, grouped by the language learned and then the language it is taught from, each with Open and Remove (in the error colour); Get a template in two formats, a standalone deck or a full language (core, layer and path), saved to Downloads; and Upload a deck. Own decks live only in the app's data on this phone and are in every backup. An upload is checked at once, and Check the deck lists what was found: problems that must be fixed, each naming its card (by its place and word, since the file's ids are not kept) or line with a fix right there (an edit in place, or one tap such as “Add quotes”), and warnings that do not block. The app gives the deck and every card new ids from a user series no course deck uses (#495), so an upload can never overlap a course; the same file uploaded again offers Replace it or Keep both. Add to my decks stays off until no problem is left. Bengali, Hindi.

3b · Review log and App log

Owner: the review log's export and import belong on “a review log screen where users can also see the logs”, and the app log is one screen, the log shown as it opens. Both put their actions in a bottom bar above Android's buttons: Review log has Export and Import, App log Copy, Export and Clear. Clear deletes, so it is in the error colour. Review log rows are the card in its script with its reading, its meaning, the activity and the time, and the rating at the end. Hindi and Bengali.

One file, fluenough-backup-<date>.fnbackup, holds the review log, every setting, reviewer mode with its rater code, and the list of downloaded languages (docs/plans/app-backup.md). Back up now saves it where the learner picks and keeps a copy here; Restore from a file opens the system picker. Cloud is set up once: the service and its folder, then a row to Cloud backups. Recent backups are the last five on the phone, whatever made them, each named by how and when, each with Download (to the phone's Downloads), Send to cloud and Restore; one already sent says “In the cloud”. Cloud backups lists every backup in the cloud, seven here, in the same rows with Download and Restore; a restore fetches the file to the phone first. Every restore shows the same sheet: what the backup holds, that the decks are not restored and will be downloaded, and that a copy of what it replaces is kept first. A file from a newer Fluenough is refused, and nothing changes. All of it is built with local backup (owner), so it is drawn live. Delete this profile leaves Your data for Your profile, opened from the card at the top; it and Who’s practising? follow the design file's New profile and Profiles screens (Fluenough app · App screens), and arrive with profiles in 0.6.0 (#204)

5 · Reminders (#90, ships in 0.6.0)

Placed now so Settings is not rearranged again. Rescoped by the owner on 2026-10-06 to one daily reminder: “streak at risk” is dropped (docs/plans/achievements.md). It comes only on days with cards due, at the time the learner picks. With Bengali and Hindi both learned it is still one reminder: it names each language with cards due and its count, or gives a fact for each (#48). The Languages row picks which learned languages it counts. Quiet hours are gone (owner, 2026-10-12: the learner sets the time). Android 13 and later ask for notifications the moment the switch is turned on, never at launch; a refusal leaves it off and says how to allow them.
