2 · The Settings tab, top to bottom

Scrolled frame by frame: each frame starts at the first row the one before could not show whole, so every row is in one frame or another. Reminders and the PIN lock are “Feature incoming” in 0.5.0, as today; section 5 draws Reminders live. Logs keeps its four rows and loses Sources. 3 · 2x text, the busiest parts

The top of the tab, then Activities and the merged data section from first row to last, at the phone's largest text size. The profile card stacks Switch under the name, as it does today above 1.3x.

4 · Backup and restore (#470), and its restore sheet

One file, fluenough-backup-<date>.fnbackup, holds the review log, every setting, reviewer mode with its rater code, and the list of downloaded languages (docs/plans/app-backup.md). Back up now saves it where the learner picks. Restore from a file opens the system picker. Below them are the backups the app made by itself as each app update downloaded, the last three, each “Before updating to {version}, {date}” with Restore. Every restore, from a file or from that list, first shows the sheet: what the backup holds, then a confirmation that it replaces everything. A file from a newer Fluenough is refused in the same sheet, and nothing changes.

5 · Reminders (#90, ships in 0.6.0)

Placed now so Settings is not rearranged again. Rescoped by the owner on 2026-10-06 to one daily reminder: “streak at risk” is dropped (docs/plans/achievements.md). It comes only on days with cards due, at the time the learner picks, says either how many cards are due or today's fact (#48), and keeps out of quiet hours. Android 13 and later ask for notifications the moment the switch is turned on, never at launch; a refusal leaves it off and says how to allow them.

6 · Every switch, on and off (#243)

From docs/plans/settings-wording.md and app_en.arb; the two new activity switches' lines are Proposed. The self-check confirms no switch keeps the same line in both states.

7 · Decisions made here, for approval

#DecisionWhy
1Proposed The merged section is called Your data and holds, in order: Backup and restore (a row to its page), Export review log, Import review log, Back up to the cloud, Delete this profile.The name people already know, and short enough for 2x text. The whole-app backup leads, as the thing most people want; the log export and import stay as they are (#20), with the merge-only import the plan keeps.
2Proposed Cloud backup is one incoming row, its line naming the five services, instead of five rows.Five rows that do nothing yet were a third of the old section. #112 decides how each service signs in; its page can list them.
3Proposed The Backup and restore page holds Back up now, Restore from a file, and “Made before app updates”. It does not repeat the log export and import.The page is the plan's “Settings, Backup and restore”. The section already shows the log rows one tap up.
4Proposed Restore from the list opens the same summary sheet as a file does. Its button says Replace and restore.The plan asks for the summary before anything changes, and a confirmation that names what is erased. One sheet for both ways in.
5Proposed Above 1.3x text, a row's button goes under its line: a backup's Restore, Adjust to me's Adjust (outlined at 2x only), and the times on Reminders.Beside it at 2x, “Before updating to 0.5.3, Nov 30” would get a column a word wide, and today Adjust leaves “Adjust to me” narrower than its words. The profile card already stacks Switch this way.
6Proposed The activity switches leave Learning for a section of their own, Activities, under it.With two more switches (#243) Learning would hold 17 rows. ADR-0034 calls them activities: “what you do, and what Settings switches on”.
7Proposed The two activities that are not skills get their switches after the skills and before Phonemic contrasts: Matching pairs and Choosing answers, wording in section 6.The skills keep today's order, which the Today tiles and the summary rows share.
8Proposed Add deck is the only row in a new Advanced section, before Logs. Icon and name are the Decks button's (+, “Add deck”), and its line is the import page's “A .yaml deck on this phone”.The owner called it advanced (#475). Beside Languages I'm learning it would sit among the choices everyone makes. Logs, also for reports, follows it.
9Proposed The daily reminder's switch and time move onto a Reminders page. Settings keeps one row, its line saying what is set, and it stays in Reminder and privacy.Time, what it says and quiet hours are four more rows; the settings-wording rule says a row that opens a page shows its value.
10Proposed Reminder kinds after the rescope: the reminder comes only on days with cards due (the approved line says so), and says either the cards due or today's fact. Quiet hours hold back a reminder the phone missed (after a restart or a time-zone change) until they end.#90's kinds without “streak at risk”. With one reminder a day at a time the learner picks, quiet hours matter only for a late one.
11Proposed A refused notification permission: the switch stays off, its line says Android blocks the notifications, and a row under it opens Android's settings for Fluenough.#90: “a refusal is handled”. Speaking does the same with the microphone today, as a message.
12Proposed The help line under Your data now points to Backup and restore; “Version {version} is available” adds that a backup is made with the download.The old line said “Export it”; the automatic backup should not be a surprise.
