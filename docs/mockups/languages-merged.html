Things to try

  • Open Hindustani: Speak and Hear are known, Read and Write are known in Devanagari and being learned in Perso-Arabic (Urdu).
  • Tap I’m learning it on Speak in a language you do not learn yet: a download is offered first. Know never downloads.
  • Tap a chosen option again to untick it. Untick everything and close the sheet: the language leaves the list (Undo in the snackbar).
  • Stop learning keeps what you know; Delete data asks for the name.
  • Add a language, search “urdu”, “বাংলা” or “east”, tick skills, then Add.
