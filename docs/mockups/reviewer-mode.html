Reviewer mode: review in the app, send by mail

The flow settled with the owner on 2026-10-09 (docs/plans/deck-browser.md, "Review in the app, by mail"), with the offensive-words rules from docs/plans/offensive-words.md. Reviewers review on the screens that already show decks and cards; nothing new but sheets and one dialog. The review leaves the phone only through the reviewer's own mail app; the public issue the workflow opens says who sent a review and for which language, never what it says. Colours are Forest (ColorScheme.fromSeed(#3F6C51)), radii and button sizes from lib/ui/theme.dart, type is the phone's system font. Words come from decks/te/te-en-family.yaml; the example sentence, the picture, the offensive card and the sound-alike pair are illustrations, since no Telugu card has them yet.
