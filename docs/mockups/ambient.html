Adjusted to you, where reviews are counted

Approach "ambient": once a skill is fitted to the learner, every place that counts reviews says so, and keeps saying so. Today's due card carries an "Adjusted to you" strip with the 30-day change; each fitted skill tile carries a small mark, "Fewer reviews" or "More reviews". For a few days after a refit the strip and the changed tiles are drawn louder, in the owner's own words. Progress, in Your strengths, explains each skill: the gap after a right answer, before and after, and the reviews over the next 30 days. Settings holds "Adjust to me" and the automatic option. Light theme on the left; dark with Pure black on the right. Colours are the Forest scheme from ColorScheme.fromSeed(#3F6C51) and the fixed skill colours in ModeColors.
