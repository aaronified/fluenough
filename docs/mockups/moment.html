"Adjusted to you": the moment

After a refit, Today shows one calm card for each skill whose schedule changed, at the top, above the lesson. It stays until the learner dismisses it or follows it to Progress. Progress keeps the lasting state for every skill under Your strengths. A refit started by hand in Settings shows its result straight away in a sheet, so no Today card follows.
