# Plan: explain the mistake on a wrong grammar answer

Written 2026-10-05. **The owner called this very important.**

## What the owner asked

> Explain my mistake (Duolingo): show the relevant rule on a wrong answer:
> for grammar questions. this is very important

## What exists

- **Grammar tables:** 80 tables, 2,497 cells. Each table has one general
  note, such as the present's "the verb's stem, then -ता, -ती or -ते for the
  doer's gender and number…". It shows after any answer, right or wrong,
  beside the table.
- **Grammar sentences:** 1,021 cards, 767 with a note of their own, such as
  "पी is feminine because चाय is, whoever drank it." The note shows after
  any answer in the typed and choice drills.
- **The word-order drill** shows no note at all.
- **Nothing ties a wrong answer to its rule.** The learner gets the answer
  and the general note, never what went wrong in what they wrote.

## What it takes

1. **Read the mistake from the table**, with no new content:
   - a wrong answer that is the form of another cell of the same table
     says which. For example: "That is the form for तुम. For मैं, जाना is
     जाता हूँ." Then it gives the rule that tells them apart;
   - a wrong answer that is the word uninflected says that it is the
     dictionary form, and what it needs.

   This covers all 2,497 cells.
2. **Rules a card can name:**
   - a grammar deck lists its rules, each with a permanent id and a short
     text;
   - a table cell or a sentence names the rules it tests;
   - a wrong answer shows those rules first, and the deck's general note
     after.

   This changes the deck format, which needs the owner's answer
   (AGENTS.md).
3. **Known mistakes, written for each sentence:** common wrong answers, each
   with why it is wrong. For hi-0480: पिया, "चाय is feminine, so पी". They
   double as the extra tiles of the word-order plan (`word-order-tiles.md`),
   so each extra tile explains itself when it is used.
4. **Everywhere a grammar answer is wrong:** typed, chosen, in word order
   and spoken. After a right answer, the note stays as it is today.
5. **Content:**
   - rules for the 80 tables;
   - rules named on the 1,021 sentences, from the 767 notes that exist,
     with the rest written;
   - known mistakes for the sentences, by agents, marked for review.
6. **Tests** for every kind of mistake read from a table, rules shown before
   the note, known mistakes, and each drill.

## To decide

- Whether the rules are written in the learner's language, as facts are
  (en, bn, hi), or in English first.
