## What it is to cover

- **The path, like Duolingo's,** winding down the screen, each deck a
  slightly rounded rectangle, not a small circle.
- **A little playful.**
- **Achievements on the path:** the CEFR levels (A1, A2, B1, from the B1
  plans' `milestone:` marks) are achievements.
- **New milestones** to be designed, such as 100 words learnt.
- Related: `achievements.md` (#223) plans badges and a summary; this plan
  places them on the path.
