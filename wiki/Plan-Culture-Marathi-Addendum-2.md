## How this addendum was checked

Every fact below was read on the page named in its row on 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16 (raw wikitext through `index.php?action=raw`, rendered pages, category pages and Wikisource search pages), with 10 to 20 seconds between requests to one host and a retry after each 429. **Nothing was written from memory.** An excerpt is copied from the page and is at most 25 words; the builder copies it again from the page when building, and runs `tools/transcribe.py` for `reading` and `ipa`.

Rights floor, as in the main plan: a named author must have died in 1955 or earlier (read on a named page); an anonymous work needs a first publication before 1900 (read on a named page). The Wikisource footer line "Creative Commons Attribution/Share-Alike 4.0" (read on every Wikisource page) is the site's licence for the page's wiki text, not a rights proof for the poem. The rights signal used is the page's own template or category, where it has one, stated per row.

## Wikisource's public-domain category
