### 2.1 Spanish conventions every ES task below carries

- Conventions of the existing Spanish decks: Latin script, no `reading`; `ipa` from `python3 tools/transcribe.py es "<text>"` (Spain pronunciation, `θ`); meanings in the layer; `native` capitalised, inverted marks kept on the target; a target starts with a capital and ends with its mark. No `phonetic` and no `phonetic_source` on any new card (ES-041 writes them).
- Header of a genre deck: as `ja-culture-1` with `language: { code: "es", iso639_3: "spa", name: "Spanish", script: "latin", tts: "es-ES", icon: "Es" }`, `part: "core"`, `kind: "vocab"`, `genre`, `level`, `tags: ["unreviewed"]`, `license: "CC-BY-SA-4.0"`; layer `decks/es/en/es-en-<name>.yaml` with `native`, `bases` meanings and note text.
- **RULE-CULT-ES** (template `decks/ja/ja-culture-1.yaml`, `wiki/Plan-Culture-Japanese.md` "Rules applied", adapted): 18 items per deck, short first, level A2 or B1; basis `public-domain` only, no `quotation` items; `source` block as batch 1 (`author`, `work`, `date`, `edition`, `basis`, `author_died`, `jurisdiction`, `rights_checked`); the excerpt is one verse, one sentence or one line, at most 25 words; text in modern spelling where the cited edition's is archaic, saying so in `edition`. **Rights floor:** an author's death year must be 1955 or earlier (the validator floor) and, for a Spanish author (`jurisdiction: "ES"`, life plus 80 for deaths before 1987), 1945 or earlier; an anonymous work needs a first publication before 1900. A work between those years goes to the reserve with a review-queue row. Dates, texts and lifespans are read by the builder from the author's or work's Wikipedia page (es.wikipedia first); what it could not read is dropped and replaced from the reserve, never filled in. Each creator: short A2-level English bio, `born`/`died` (`circa` if uncertain), era, one legacy line with a checkable item and a source link, no image.
- **RULE-PV-ES** (template `ja-proverbs-1`): `genre: "proverbs"`, `basis: "oral-tradition"`, `source` block with `collection`, `date` (the collection's), `edition`, `jurisdiction`, `rights_checked`; one `literal` note per card and a meaning; level A2 or B1; a refrán whose text the builder did not read on the cited page is dropped.
- **RULE-MW-ES** (template `ja-multiword-te-auxiliaries`): `genre: "multiword"`, a `pattern_family`, `level`, a `usage` note on every card, `words` and `bases` for each part, parts already taught written by `ref`, a part not yet a card gets a card.
- **RULE-NOTE** as in the Deck-Work-Queue, with Spanish words needing no reading (the Latin prose just names the form).
- Candidate lists below name works and creators only. ES-012 turns them into the wiki sourcing page; if the page and a row differ, the page wins.

### 3.1 Telugu conventions every TE task below carries

- Header of a core: as `te-food-sentences` (`schema: 1`, `id`, `part: "core"`, `kind: "vocab"`, `language: { code: "te", iso639_3: "tel", name: "Telugu", script: "telugu", tts: "te-IN", icon: "తె" }`, `license: "CC-BY-SA-4.0"`, `authors`, `tags: ["beginner", "unreviewed"]`); layer `decks/te/en/te-en-<name>.yaml` with `kind: "layer"`, `core`, `native`, `name`, `description` ("Not yet checked by a Telugu speaker: please report any mistake."), `cards:` keyed by id.
- Every new card has `reading` and `ipa` from `python3 tools/transcribe.py te "<Telugu>" "<typed Latin hint of how it is said>"` (the tool reads the hint against the letters). Readings are based on ISO 15919 (never say strictly); a word-final anusvara is written `ṁ` (owner, 2026-10-19). No `phonetic`, no `phonetic_source` (TE-058 writes them). A doubtful reading or spelling goes to the review queue, never guessed.
- **Sentence cards** (shape of `te-food-sentences`): `id`, `target`, `reading`, `ipa`, `pos: "phrase"`, `rules: ["te-rule-..."]` where the sentence practises a rule of a `te-grammar-*` rules deck at or before the unit (the builder reads the rule ids from those decks; none is invented), `bases` with `ref` for every word that is a card (`{ word, ref }`) and `{ word, base, reading }` for an inflected form or a closed-class word; layer gives `native` (one plain English sentence), `alt_native` where another natural rendering exists, `bases` meanings (plain English, no Telugu letters), notes where the sentence has one point (RULE-NOTE: Latin prose, every Telugu word followed by its reading in parentheses).
- **Sentence gate for Telugu** (the Deck-Work-Queue's rules 1 to 10 and variety rules, adapted): a whole sentence (a finite verb, or a complete nominal sentence with no copula as Telugu allows), 3 to 8 words counted by spaces (Telugu packs more into a word), ending with `.` or `?`; faithful full translation, same mood both sides; taught words only (every stem is a card of a deck at or before the task's unit, inflections are those the rules decks teach; a word not taught is never used and never added); no template text; Telugu script only in the target; unique target and meaning across the course (the builder searches the language's decks first); strict frames (no two sentences of a deck share a skeleton or differ by one word); verb cap by the pool P of verbs taught up to the unit (P of 13 or more: no verb more than twice; P of 8 to 12: at most three times; P under 8: the unit is skipped and reported); 10 sentences per deck (8 if the unit holds under 20 words), never padded, about 2 questions and 8 statements, at least 3 shapes, at least min(5, P) different verbs, no content noun more than twice; shuffled so no run of three shares a shape or mood.
- **Reading decks** (shape of `hi-en-reading-agriculture` and `te-en-reading-diddubatu`, but written for the deck): `kind: "reading"`, `source: "Written for this deck"`, `tags: ["reading", "unreviewed"]`, one passage per id the path names under the unit's `reading_passages` (the passage `id` is that id; `title` is the path's English text; `theme`), 4 sentences per passage each `{ text, reading, ipa }`, 3 questions per passage (`id` from the task's range, `prompt { en }`, either `answer: true/false` or two `options` and a 1-based `answer`), all words taught up to the unit; the path edit adds `te-reading-<stem>` to the unit's `decks` (the file is `te-en-reading-<stem>`).
- **RULE-CULT-TE** (as RULE-CULT-ES): 18 items per deck, `public-domain` only; text **as printed in the cited edition** in Telugu script (a classical poem is not modernised), every archaic form listed in the layer with its modern form (as the glossary of `te-en-reading-diddubatu`); `reading` and `ipa` by the tool; death year 1955 or earlier (the validator floor; India's life plus 60 is later, so the floor rules); an anonymous work first published before 1900; excerpt one verse or line, at most 25 words; every date, text and lifespan read by the builder from te.wikipedia or en.wikipedia and a cited Wikisource edition, what was not read is dropped and replaced from the reserve; level B1 unless the item is short and plain (A2). Creators: `decks/creators/te.yaml` with eras (Kakatiya, Vijayanagara, Nayaka, colonial, twentieth century).
- **RULE-PV-TE** and **RULE-MW-TE**: as the Spanish rules with Telugu fields; a multiword card's citation form is the verbal noun in -ḍaṁ (`రావడం`, as the course's verb decks).
- No Telugu speaker reviews (owner, 2026-11-13); everything is `unreviewed`, doubts go to `docs/review-queue/te.md`.
