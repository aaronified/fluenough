# Learner guide

Fluenough is a drilling app for the basics of a language: words, saying them, hearing them and a little grammar. Each answer you give decides when that word comes back. It runs offline once a language's decks are downloaded. There is no account.

This guide says what you see and what to do. It does not cover how the app is built: for that see [Architecture](Architecture).

## The four tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Today | The day's lesson, the words due, quick revision and a fact of the day. |
| Decks | Your courses, their paths, units and decks. |
| Progress | Reviews, what you remember, your streak. |
| Settings | Sound, look, backups, updates and more. |

## Features that are not on yet

Some features are built into the screens but not switched on in a version. They are shown dimmed, with a "Feature incoming" badge, and tapping them says "Feature incoming. Not in this version yet." This is on purpose ([ADR-0008](ADR-0008-Unbuilt-Features-Are-Shown-Disabled)). The [Roadmap](Roadmap) says what is coming and when.

## Text size

The app makes text bigger up to 1.4 times the normal size ([#505](https://github.com/aaronified/fluenough/issues/505)). If your phone is set larger, the app is drawn at 1.4 times. Card text can be made larger on its own: see [Profiles and settings](Profiles-and-Settings).
