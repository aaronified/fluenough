## The licence in detail

Fluenough is free software and every fork must stay free software. The
exception exists solely so the app can be distributed through stores whose
terms would otherwise conflict with the GPL; it grants no permission to close
the source. See [LICENSE](https://github.com/aaronified/fluenough/blob/main/LICENSE) and
[LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md](https://github.com/aaronified/fluenough/blob/main/LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md), and
[ADR-0003](ADR-0003-Licence) for the reasoning.

Deck content is **CC BY-SA 4.0**, with attribution, declared in each deck's
`license` ([ADR-0040](ADR-0040-Decks-Are-Cc-By-Sa)). Data taken
from Wiktionary, Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia or Tatoeba names its source,
and the app credits them under Settings, Sources.
